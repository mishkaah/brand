### Colors & tokens

- **Obsidian Dark** (`#09090B`): Calm background for night reading.
- **Gold Accent** (`#E5CFA0` → `#B89655`): Warm calligraphic accent.
- **Parchment Paper** (`#F6F3EC`): Manuscript paper tone for day reading.
